## Overview

**Zoho CRM** is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform for small businesses, startups, growing companies, and sales teams.

It helps businesses manage leads, contacts, sales pipelines, customer communication, workflow automation, and reports.

## Key Features

- Lead management
- Contact management
- Sales automation
- Workflow automation
- Email and communication tools
- Reports and dashboards
- Multichannel CRM
- AI assistant features
